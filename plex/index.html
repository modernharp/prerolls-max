Intro videos on Plex

No plugin, no Plex Pass. Prerolls Max keeps Plex's own intro list up to date.

Plex has a setting for intro videos called Cinema Trailers, but it takes one fixed list. To change an intro for Halloween, you'd edit that list by hand and remember to change it back. Prerolls Max edits it for you. When a schedule starts or ends, the list changes.

What you need

Plex Media Server, Prerolls Max running, and your intro videos in a folder Plex can open. No Plex Pass and no plugin.

Set it up

  1. Install Prerolls Max

    Use the Windows installer or the Docker image, open it in a browser at port 9393, and make your admin account.

  2. Sign in with Plex

    Open Media Servers and press Connect Plex, then Sign in with Plex. A Plex window opens. Your password goes to Plex, never to Prerolls Max. Come back and Prerolls Max finds your server.

    If no window opens, your browser blocked the pop-up: press Open the Plex sign-in page instead. You can also connect with the server address and a token under Other ways to connect.

  3. Turn on the Plex settings

    Enable Cinema Trailers in each movie library: edit the library, open Advanced. In each Plex app, Cinema trailers to play before movies must not be 0. On the 2026 Plex apps for Apple TV, iPhone, iPad, Android TV and Fire TV, set it to 1, or those apps skip intros.

  4. Add videos and choose when they play

    Add your own intros or get free ones in Intro Videos, put them in collections, then press New schedule under When They Play. Turn on default intros so a movie never starts without one. Getting started covers each step.

  5. Test it

    On Home, press Start test next to Play a test, then start a movie from the beginning in a web browser. Your intro should play first. Test in a browser before a TV or phone: some apps skip intros until you change a setting. See which apps play intros.

What Plex can and can't do

  • Plex plays intros before movies only, never before TV episodes.
  • Plex has one intro list for the whole server. Everyone watching at the same time gets intros from the same list.
  • Intros don't play when you resume a movie.
  • Casting, DLNA, Plexamp, downloads and Infuse never play intros.

Plex questions

Does Plex need a plugin for prerolls?

No. Prerolls Max fills in Plex's own Cinema Trailers setting and keeps it up to date.

Do I need Plex Pass?

No. Plex plays your own intro videos without Plex Pass.

Can Plex play a different intro for each movie or person?

No. Plex has one intro list for the whole server and picks the intro before it knows which movie is starting. Prerolls Max changes the list when a schedule starts or ends, which works well for dates and holidays. For choices by genre, rating or person, use Jellyfin or Emby.

Why are there no intros before TV episodes on Plex?

Plex plays intros before movies only. This is a Plex limit, not a setting.

Why can't Plex find my intro files?

Prerolls Max sends Plex the path to each file, and Plex opens the file itself. In Docker the two may see the folder under different paths. Folder mapping fixes it, with a full example.
