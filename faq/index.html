Questions people ask

Short answers. Each one links to the full guide when there is one.

Is Prerolls Max free?

Yes, for use at home and other noncommercial purposes. The licence is PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0, which does not allow selling it or using it to make money. The source code is public on GitHub.

What is a preroll?

A short video that plays before the movie, like the clips a cinema shows before the main film. Plex calls the setting Cinema Trailers. Jellyfin and Emby call them intros.

Which media servers does it work with?

Plex, Jellyfin and Emby, and you can connect more than one at once. Plex needs no plugin. Jellyfin (10.11 and 12) and Emby (4.9 and later) use a small Prerolls Max plugin. See the pages for Plex, Jellyfin and Emby.

Do I need Plex Pass?

No. Plex plays your own intro videos without Plex Pass.

Do I need Emby Premiere?

Yes. Emby plays intros through its Cinema Intros feature, and that needs Emby Premiere.

Where does it run?

On your own computer or NAS: a Windows 10 or 11 PC with the installer, or any machine that runs Docker (including Unraid, Synology and Raspberry Pi). Your intro videos stay in a folder you choose. Download options.

Why does everyone get the same intro on Plex?

Plex has one intro list for the whole server, not one per person. Prerolls Max changes that list when a schedule starts or ends. If two people start a movie at the same time, they get intros from the same list. Jellyfin and Emby pick an intro for each play.

Can intros play before TV episodes?

On Jellyfin and Emby, yes, with the Prerolls Max plugin. They play before the episode you pick, not between episodes that follow each other on their own. On Plex, no: Plex plays intros before movies only.

Can I play a different intro for each genre, age rating or person?

On Jellyfin and Emby, yes, with rules. The right intro is picked for each play. On Plex, no: Plex picks its intro before it knows which movie is starting.

Why didn't the intro play when I carried on watching?

No media server plays an intro when you resume a movie. Start it from the beginning and the intro plays.

Which video format should I use?

MP4 with H.264 video and AAC sound plays everywhere. Most intros run 10 to 30 seconds. If an intro is 4K or HDR and your movies are not, make a TV-friendly version in Prerolls Max.

Why does my Roku play only the first intro?

The official Jellyfin app on Roku plays only the first intro in the list. Combine your intros into one video and it plays them all.

I use NeXroll. Can I keep my setup?

Yes. Prerolls Max replaces NeXroll and brings your schedules, collections and settings across. Stop NeXroll first: both use port 9393 and both would change the same media servers. Step-by-step guide.

My intro isn't playing. What do I check?

Play a movie from the beginning in a web browser first. If the intro plays there, your setup works and the app on your TV or phone is the reason. The full checklist covers each case, most common first.
