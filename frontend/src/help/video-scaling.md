# Video scaling

Media servers often convert a big intro while it plays. A 4K intro before a movie someone watches away from home can stutter, and some apps skip it. A smaller copy plays straight away.

[Settings, Advanced, Video scaling](app:/settings/advanced/scaling) makes that copy:

1. Under **Video**, pick an intro. The page shows its size now.
2. Under **New size**, pick the size you want. 720p suits watching away from home; 480p suits slow connections.
3. Choose **Keep both** to add the copy next to the original, or **Replace the original** to save space. A replaced original can't be brought back, so download a backup first if you're not sure.
4. Press **Convert**.

A short intro converts in under a minute. If a video is already that size, nothing changes.

Most people never need this. Try it if an intro plays at home but not on a phone away from home. To fix HDR or 4K intros for TVs at home, use **Make TV-friendly version** on Intro Videos instead. See [Even loudness, TV-friendly versions and combined videos](help:media-tools).
