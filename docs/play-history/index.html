What Played shows which intros played, before which movie or episode, for whom and on which app. On a phone, open it from the link on Home.

At the top, pick a time (the last 7, 30 or 90 days, or the last year) and a server.

  • Recent plays lists the newest first.
  • Played most and Played least cover the time you picked.
  • Never played lists intros with no play recorded. They're worth a look: maybe they're in a collection nothing plays, or they could use a higher How often.
  • By server, By person and By app split the plays up.

Keeping and clearing history#

History older than 90 days is forgotten on its own. To change that, or to turn history off, open Settings, Playback. Without history, What Played, the missed-intro check and "don't repeat within" can't work.

Clear history removes everything at once. Only an admin can do it. "Don't repeat within" uses this history, so after clearing it a recent intro may play again soon.

Backups leave play history out unless you turn on Include play history in backups in Settings.

On Jellyfin and Emby a play counts when the server asks for intros. An app that asks and then doesn't play them still counts, but that's rare. The missed-intro check finds apps that don't ask at all.
