# Words used in Prerolls Max

**Admin:** A person who can change everything in Prerolls Max, including settings and who can sign in. The first account is always an admin.

**API key:** A long code that lets one program talk to another without a password. Jellyfin and Emby give you one to paste into Prerolls Max. Prerolls Max can make keys for other apps too, under Settings, Advanced, API keys.

**Binge, first episode of a:** The first episode someone starts after a break of a few hours (4 unless you change it). Rules can play intros only before that one.

**Cinema Intros:** Emby's name for its intro feature. It needs Emby Premiere, and it has to be on in the server's settings and in each Emby app.

**Cinema Mode:** The Jellyfin app setting that plays intros. It's on in most apps, but off to begin with in the Roku app.

**Cinema Trailers:** Plex's name for its intro and trailer setting. Each movie library needs **Enable Cinema Trailers** on, and each Plex app needs **Cinema trailers to play before movies** set to at least 1.

**Collection:** A group of intro videos, like "Halloween". Schedules and rules play collections.

**Combined video:** Several intros joined into one video, for apps that play only the first intro, like Jellyfin on Roku.

**Community:** Free intros made by other people, from Prerolls.me and Prerolls.uk. Find them on Intro Videos, under **Get more from Community**.

**Default intros:** The intros that play when no schedule is on, so a movie always gets one.

**Docker:** A way to run programs in separate boxes called containers. Each container sees your folders under its own names, which is why folder mapping exists.

**Don't repeat within:** A setting on a collection or rule that keeps the same intro from playing again within a number of hours.

**Folder mapping:** A setting that tells Prerolls Max how your media server names the intro folder, when the two see it under different paths.

**How often:** A setting from 1 to 10 on each intro. Higher numbers play more often when intros are picked at random.

**If schedules overlap:** What happens when two schedules are on at the same time: the more important one plays, the intros are mixed, or only one schedule's intros play.

**Intro video:** A short video that plays before a movie. Also called a preroll or pre-roll.

**Kids-safe:** A title whose age rating is on your kids-safe list, like G, PG, TV-Y or U. Rules can play kids' intros before these.

**Loudness, LUFS:** How loud a video sounds overall, measured in LUFS. Lower numbers are quieter. Prerolls Max aims for -23 LUFS, the usual level for TV.

**Media server:** The program that holds your movies and streams them to your TV: Plex, Jellyfin or Emby.

**Missed-intro check:** Prerolls Max watching what your media servers play, and warning on Home when a movie started without its intro.

**Movie trailers:** Trailers for upcoming movies that play before your film, like at the cinema. Found under Advanced.

**Never-play list:** Titles that never get an intro, like concert films. Works on Jellyfin and Emby.

**NeXroll:** The app Prerolls Max started from. NeXroll's categories are now collections, filler is default intros, sequences are playlists, and NeX-Up is movie trailers.

**Next play only:** On Plex, some choices can only change the intro list before the next movie someone starts, not for each person or each title.

**No-intro tag:** Tag a movie or show `no-intro` in Jellyfin or Emby and it never gets an intro.

**Playlist:** A set order of intros, like a cinema pre-show: a welcome, then a trailer, then a "turn off your phone" clip. Found under Advanced.

**Plex Pass, Emby Premiere:** Paid upgrades for Plex and Emby. Plex intros don't need Plex Pass. Emby's Cinema Intros needs Emby Premiere.

**Plugin:** A small add-on for Jellyfin or Emby. It asks Prerolls Max which intro to play each time something starts.

**Preroll:** Another word for an intro video.

**Request ID, error code:** A short code shown with some errors, like `a1b2c3d4`. It points to the exact entry in the logs. Include it when you report a problem.

**Rule:** "When this kind of title starts, play these intros." For example, kids' intros before anything rated PG. Works on Jellyfin and Emby.

**Schedule:** When a collection plays, like "every year from October 1 to October 31".

**Token:** Plex's version of an API key. Signing in with Plex gets one for you.

**Trash:** Where deleted videos wait for 30 days before they're removed for good.

**TV-friendly version:** A copy of an intro in 1080p, without HDR, that every app plays as it is.

**Video maker:** A tool for making simple intro videos from text and pictures. Found under Advanced.

**Watch folder:** A folder Prerolls Max keeps an eye on. New videos put there are added to a collection on their own.

**What Played:** The page that shows which intros played, when, for whom and on which app.
