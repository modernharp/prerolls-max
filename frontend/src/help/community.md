# Community intros

The **Get more from Community** tab on [Intro Videos](app:/videos/community) has free intros made by other people. They come from two libraries: Prerolls.me at the top, and Prerolls.uk below it. You can pick from both at once.

- **Prerolls.me** has about 600 intros, each credited to the person who made it. About 85 can be downloaded here. The rest are on YouTube or Vimeo, so you can watch them but not download them.
- **Prerolls.uk**, run by Typical Nerds, has about 1,800 intros, many in separate versions for Plex, Jellyfin and Emby. All of them can be downloaded.

## The first time you open Prerolls.uk

Prerolls.uk asks you to agree to its fair-use terms first. In short: the videos are for your own media server, not to sell or share somewhere else. Read them, then press **I agree**. Prerolls Max remembers your answer.

Then press **Get the list**. Prerolls Max downloads the list of videos once. It takes about a minute, with a progress bar. After that the list is kept, and **Check for new videos** updates it whenever you like.

## Downloading

1. Tick **Select** on each video you want.
2. Press **Download** in the bar at the bottom.
3. Under **Add them to**, choose a collection, or make a new one.

The videos download together, with a progress bar. When it's done, the window says what was added and what you already had. The new videos are in **Your videos**, and the community list marks them **In your library**.

## Videos you can only watch

Videos hosted on YouTube or Vimeo show **Watch on YouTube** (or Vimeo) instead of **Select**. Press the picture to watch one here. Prerolls Max doesn't download from YouTube or Vimeo. If the creator offers a download on their own page, download it there, then upload it on Intro Videos.

## Credits

Each video says who made it and where it's from, like "by Jane · via Prerolls.me". The credit is saved with the video when you download it. If you share a video, please keep the credit with it.

## If a library doesn't load

Each library shows its own problem and what to do, for example when the site is down for a while. The other library keeps working. Press **Check for new videos** to try again later.
