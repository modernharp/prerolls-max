You decide when each intro plays

A schedule is a set of intros plus a time. When two overlap, you choose which one wins, and a calendar shows what plays on every day.

  • All of OctoberHalloween intros. Pick dates, days of the week, or a holiday. Moving ones like Easter are built in.
  • Anything rated PGA kids' intro. Rules choose by genre, age rating, decade, library or person on Jellyfin and Emby.
  • Friday nightA cinema-style pre-show. Pick days of the week and times of day.
  • Horror from the 1980sAn 80s horror intro. A rule can match a decade and a genre together. Press Try it to check a title.
  • Everything elseYour default intros. They play when no schedule is on, so no movie starts bare.

Up and running in three steps

Most people are done in under half an hour. The app walks you through each step and checks it worked.

  1. Install it

    Run the Windows installer, or start the Docker image on a NAS, Unraid or Linux machine. It opens as a web page at port 9393.

  2. Connect your server

    On Plex you sign in and you're done. Jellyfin and Emby also need a small plugin, and the app downloads the right one for your version.

  3. Pick videos and times

    Add your own videos or get free ones from Prerolls.me and Prerolls.uk. Then press Play a test and watch it play before a movie.

What's inside

Your videos, in collections

Drop in files or paste a link. Group them into collections like "Everyday" and "Halloween", and let Prerolls Max even out the loudness so no intro is far louder than the rest. MP4 plays on every server.

Free intros, with credit

Browse Prerolls.me and Prerolls.uk without leaving the app. Each video names the person who made it. YouTube and Vimeo videos open as links and are never downloaded.

Schedules in three steps

Choose the collection, choose when, and decide what happens when two schedules overlap. A sentence on the right says what will happen, in plain words, before you save.

The whole year on a calendar

See which schedule plays on each day and which one wins when they overlap. Halloween, Christmas and your everyday intros each get their own colour.

Rules for Jellyfin and Emby

Pick a different intro by genre, age rating, decade, library or person. Keep a never-play list. Play intros only before the first episode of a binge. Try it shows which rule would match a title, and why.

All three servers, side by side

Plex, Jellyfin and Emby each get a card that shows whether the connection and the plugin are working. Next to every option, a badge tells you what that server can do.

Help for when an intro doesn't play

A troubleshooter asks which server and app you use and checks your setup as you answer. Home also warns you when a movie started without its intro. The guides are built in and work offline.

Dark mode, and your phone

The app follows your system's light or dark setting. On a phone the same pages fit the screen, with a tab bar at the bottom.

Coming from NeXroll?

Prerolls Max started as a fork of NeXroll 2.4.0 and replaces it. Run one or the other, not both: Plex has one intro list per server and both apps would keep overwriting it, and on Jellyfin and Emby every movie would get two sets of intros.

Your schedules, collections and settings can come across. Open Settings, then Moving from NeXroll, and Prerolls Max reads NeXroll's data folder, a backup file, or a NeXroll that's still running. It shows what it found before it changes anything.

Questions people ask

Is Prerolls Max free?

Yes, for use at home and other noncommercial purposes. The licence is PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0, which does not allow selling it or using it to make money. The source code is public on GitHub.

What is a preroll?

A short video that plays before the movie, like the clips a cinema shows before the main film. Plex calls the setting Cinema Trailers. Jellyfin and Emby call them intros.

Which media servers does it work with?

Plex, Jellyfin and Emby, and you can connect more than one at once. Plex needs no plugin. Jellyfin (10.11 and 12) and Emby (4.9 and later) use a small Prerolls Max plugin. See the pages for Plex, Jellyfin and Emby.

Why does everyone get the same intro on Plex?

Plex has one intro list for the whole server, not one per person. Prerolls Max changes that list when a schedule starts or ends. If two people start a movie at the same time, they get intros from the same list. Jellyfin and Emby pick an intro for each play.

I use NeXroll. Can I keep my setup?

Yes. Prerolls Max replaces NeXroll and brings your schedules, collections and settings across. Stop NeXroll first: both use port 9393 and both would change the same media servers. Step-by-step guide.

My intro isn't playing. What do I check?

Play a movie from the beginning in a web browser first. If the intro plays there, your setup works and the app on your TV or phone is the reason. The full checklist covers each case, most common first.
