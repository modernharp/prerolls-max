Prerolls Max

Intro videos (prerolls) for Plex, Jellyfin and Emby. Prerolls Max replaces NeXroll.

Jellyfin plugin repository

In Jellyfin, open Dashboard, Plugins, Repositories, and add the one for your server:

  • Jellyfin 12: https://modernharp.github.io/prerolls-max/jellyfin/manifest.json
  • Jellyfin 10.11: https://modernharp.github.io/prerolls-max/jellyfin/manifest-10.11.json

Other downloads

  • Releases: Windows installer, Emby plugin, checksums
  • Docker: docker pull ghcr.io/modernharp/prerolls-max:latest
  • Unraid template: https://modernharp.github.io/prerolls-max/unraid/prerolls-max.xml
